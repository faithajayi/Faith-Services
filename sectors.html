Selected client case studies · Verified outcomes

Successful bids. Stronger routes to public sector work.

Examples of procurement outcomes achieved by clients with tender support from Faith Services. We focus on the documented result and explain what that result means in practice.

HEALTH & SOCIAL CARE · HAMPSHIRE

Fastrack Resources Limited – Help 2 Live at Home framework

Procurement outcome: Successful admission to Hampshire County Council’s AS24587 – Help 2 Live at Home (2024) framework.

Faith Services supported the provider with its tender submission for the council’s home-care framework. The successful result secured the business a place on the framework, providing an approved route to be considered for relevant care service opportunities under its terms.

Result: successful framework admission.

LIVE-IN CARE · SOUTHAMPTON

Mimarcare Limited – Southampton live-in care platform

Procurement outcome: Southampton City Council confirmed the award of Mimarcare Limited onto its Innovative Platform for Home Based Services, reference EC09-01-3216, for Lot 2 – Live-in Care.

The council’s award notification, dated 17 April 2026, confirms that Mimarcare’s submission met the relevant Stage 1 criteria and the Stage 2 Minimum Quality Requirements. The award covers the platform’s short-term, medium-term and long-term live-in care categories for the stated branch location.

Faith Services supported the tender process and preparation of the submission. Being admitted to the platform gives the provider an opportunity to be invited to tender for specific requirements; individual care packages are awarded through subsequent processes.

Result: successful award onto Southampton City Council’s Lot 2 Live-in Care platform.

CLEANING & FACILITIES · SOUTH WEST

Mckoy’s Enterprise Ltd – Advantage South West Cleaning DPS

Procurement outcome: Successful application and award of a place on the Advantage South West Dynamic Purchasing System (DPS) for Cleaning & Associated Services.

The acceptance letter, dated 14 April 2026, confirms that Mckoy’s Enterprise Ltd was awarded a place on the DPS for contracts up to £50,000 per annum. Faith Services supported the application that secured the company’s place on this procurement route.

Membership allows the business to be invited to tender for specific cleaning requirements issued by participating members. Each contract is still subject to its own competition and evaluation.

Result: successful DPS application – eligible to compete for cleaning opportunities up to £50k per annum.

HEALTH & SOCIAL CARE · HAMPSHIRE

Mimarcare Ltd – Help 2 Live at Home framework

SECURITY SERVICES · COMMERCIAL

UValue Facilities Ltd – security services contract

Client outcome: The business reported securing a security services contract following tender support from Faith Services.

We supported UValue Facilities Ltd with its move into contract opportunities in the security services sector. The client reported a successful contract win. The buyer, contract title, value and award date have not been included because those details were not supplied for publication.

Result: successful contract win reported by the client.

How to read these results: Framework or DPS admission means a supplier has been accepted onto a procurement route and may be invited to compete for specific contracts. It does not mean that a specific call-off contract or guaranteed revenue has been awarded. The Southampton City Council and Advantage South West outcomes above are described in line with the uploaded award notifications. Client names and outcomes should be published only where the relevant client permissions are in place.

How pricing works

Clear scope before work begins.

Deposits & payment

Our standard payment arrangement is a 50% deposit before work starts, with the remaining 50% payable before final delivery.

Revisions & deadlines

Included revisions depend on the package agreed. Urgent deadlines may attract an additional fee, which will be confirmed in your quote.

Custom quotations

Complex frameworks, multiple lots, bespoke documentation and larger projects are scoped and priced individually.

All prices are shown in GBP (£). VAT will be added where applicable. Government, regulator, filing, statutory and third-party professional fees are separate unless expressly included in a written quotation. Services are support and preparation services; they do not guarantee a tender award, grant award, CQC registration, certification or other regulatory or commercial outcome. CQC regulates relevant activities in England; other UK nations have their own care regulators. Legal, tax and regulated professional advice should be obtained from an appropriately qualified professional where required.
